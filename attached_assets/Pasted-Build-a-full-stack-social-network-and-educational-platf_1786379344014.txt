Build a full-stack social network and educational platform called "AIMD Network" — powered by the Art Is My Drug (AIMD) 501(c)(3) non-profit. It serves as our owned social media home for artists and creators, combining daily habit tracking, community feeds, workshop education, event organizing, and studio career pipelines.

### Key Functional Hubs & Social Features:

1. Owned Social Feed & Community Hub ("The Studio Feed"):
   - Creator Profiles: Custom portfolio showcases, current art mediums, skill badges, and active daily habit streaks.
   - Studio Feed: Members post daily artwork/progress shots, WIP (work-in-progress) videos, and studio session logs.
   - Critique & Peer Review: Specialized comment sections optimized for constructive feedback (e.g., line work, composition, shading).
   - Direct & Group Messaging: Private chats and group channels for local pop-up organizing, workshop groups, and studio projects.

2. Community Events & Activation Manager:
   - Event Feed & RSVP: Display upcoming AIMD live workshops, pop-up events, gallery shows, and community activations.
   - Interactive Map / Directory: Discover local creators, alumni, and AIMD partner studio locations.

3. Phase 1 to Phase 3 Pipeline (Habits -> Workshops -> Career Placement):
   - Daily Habit Tracker with focus timers and visual momentum heatmaps.
   - Workshop Portal: Video masterclasses, assignment uploads, instructor feedback, and milestone certification badges.
   - Studio Chair & Opportunity Board: Direct application portal for certified apprentices and artists to book guest spots or fill open resident chairs at AIMD and partner studios.

4. Non-Profit & Sponsorship Engine:
   - Integrated Stripe donation and membership system allowing patrons/donors to "Sponsor a Creator" or fund community workshops.

### Technical & Design Specifications:
- Tech Stack: React (frontend), Tailwind CSS, Express (backend), PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM (database), Stripe Sandbox integration.
- UI/UX Aesthetic: High-contrast, dark-mode studio interface with bold streetwear-inspired typography, neon emerald accents, and a fast, app-like mobile experience.
