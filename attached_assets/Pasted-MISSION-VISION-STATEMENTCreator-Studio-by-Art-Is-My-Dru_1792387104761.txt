MISSION & VISION STATEMENTCreator Studio by Art Is My Drug (AIMD)Founder: Edward "Poncho" Davila (Ponchotheartist)The Founder’s Journey: From Passion to PurposeMy journey with art started genuinely through a lifelong passion discovered in high school and college. I was fortunate enough to find tattooing as a career, but art has always been my true north. By applying daily dedication, habit, and discipline to my craft, I turned passion into a professional life. Now, after more than a decade of building artists, tattooing, muralism, and hosting community cultural events in Los Angeles, I want to give back on a global scale. With Creator Studio, we are building something far more tangible and permanent than a one-off event or workshop—we are leaving a legacy and building a true backbone to support independent art.Our Core MissionTo bridge the critical gap between raw artistic talent and sustainable professional independence by instilling daily practice, connecting creators worldwide, and providing a real-world physical and digital ecosystem.   The Creator Journey & Value LadderWe meet artists wherever they are—locally, nationally, or internationally—and guide them through a structured three-stage evolution:Discovery Artists ($0 / Month):The Foundation of Daily Practice. It all starts with a habit. Through daily check-in streaks, community challenges, and public feeds, we give emerging creators the space to show up, build consistency, and find their direction.   Dedicated Artists ($39.99 / Month):Workshops, Mastery, and Support. For artists ready to deepen their craft, we offer structured video modules, technical lessons, and master support to elevate their skills across tattooing, visual arts, music, and fashion.   Professional Artists ($99.00 / Month):The Artist Resort & Professional Pipeline. For seasoned creators who have mastered their discipline and want to give back, our Professional tier unlocks advanced business pipelines, direct mentorship, and access to our physical artist resorts, retreats, and container project spaces.   Accessibility & SponsorshipsTrue art should never be locked behind financial barriers. For creators who lack resources, we bridge the accessibility gap through brand sponsorships and endorsements, ensuring that talent—not capital—determines an artist's path.
