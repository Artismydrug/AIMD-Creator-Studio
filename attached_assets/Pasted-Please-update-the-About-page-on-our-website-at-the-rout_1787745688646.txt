# About Us — Art Is My Drug

## Our Journey: From a Local Hub to a Creative Movement
Art Is My Drug started with a vision to build a true community of creators. In 2014, Edward “Ponchotheartist” Davila opened his very first shop in Torrance, CA. What started as a 1,000 sq. ft. tattoo shop quickly grew into something much bigger, expanding to over 4,000 sq. ft. and serving as a bustling hub for the community. We brought not just tattoos, but fashion, poetry, music, and all forms of art under one roof. 

Our momentum pushed us beyond the walls of a traditional shop:
- 2017: We built the first Mobile Tattoo Bus.
- National & California Tours: We collaborated on high-profile projects with Hulu, Mammoth/Heavyhitters, Coachella activations, LA Weekly, and more.

## The Next Era: Concrete Jungle & The Artist Resort
In 2024, we took a massive leap forward and moved to Vintageland/Concrete Jungle—a sprawling 100,000 sq. ft. space featuring 3 levels dedicated to creation. 

We are now scaling to build our long-time dream: The Artist Resort. Our plan is to build a sustainable compound utilizing shipping containers, and our very first container compound is already set up right here at Concrete Jungle!

## Supporting the Creators of Tomorrow
We are deeply committed to lifting up the next generation. In 2024, we officially launched our Non-Profit organization dedicated to supporting and nurturing the youth. We have already hosted over a dozen art workshops and plan to keep building the creators of tomorrow.
