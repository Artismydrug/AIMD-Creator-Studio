01Test + improve

Keep build 10 in TestFlight and turn feedback into fixes.

  • Test sign-up, sign-in, onboarding, dashboard, and profile.
  • Test habits, studio log, workshops, messages, and notifications.
  • Test challenge timing, photo/video upload, one submission, and 20-point award.
  • Fix crashes, broken uploads, confusing states, and launch blockers.
  • Review progress weekly in Creator Studio HQ + Admin Dashboard.

02Finalize

Lock the public launch experience.

  • Remove placeholder, fake, and unavailable content.
  • Finalize the first two or three workshops, instructors, dates, and signups.
  • Prepare creator stories and “Why building a creative habit matters” content.
  • Freeze launch scope and create the next iOS/iPadOS build.
  • Run final QA on web, iPhone, and iPad.

03Submit + warm up

Test the final build and prepare the public moment.

  • Upload the final build to TestFlight and complete the last tester pass.
  • Confirm App Store metadata, screenshots, privacy details, and release notes.
  • Submit the final native build to Apple App Review.
  • Confirm owner access to Creator Studio HQ and Admin Dashboard.
  • Prepare launch announcements, event details, support coverage, and content posts.

04Release + launch

Release only after the final native build is approved.

  • Release the approved iOS/iPadOS version manually around October 22.
  • Confirm web and API production behavior before public promotion.
  • Confirm Challenge 1 is upcoming until October 24 and awards 20 points.
  • Run the launch event, first-habit activation, workshop signups, and live support.
  • Review errors, signups, submissions, and feedback after launch.

05Challenge launch rules

  • Challenge 1: Where I’m From — opens October 24 and runs for two weeks.
  • Every original response earns 20 AIMD points once, regardless of medium.
  • Welcome drawings, poems, paintings, songs, dances, photos, outfits, recipes, sculptures, videos, workouts, movement pieces, and other original formats.
  • No extra points for captions, process stories, feedback, votes, popularity, or winning.

Launch blockers

Crashes · sign-in failures · broken uploads · incorrect points · missing workshop details · placeholder content · unavailable launch surfaces · failed final iOS/iPadOS build.

Can wait

Audio proof · admin-managed challenge scheduling · sponsor tools · large analytics dashboard · longer challenge seasons.
