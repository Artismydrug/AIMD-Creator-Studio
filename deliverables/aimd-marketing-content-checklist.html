Campaign 01 · Why building a creative habit matters

Creative habits turn inspiration into practice, practice into confidence, and confidence into a body of work. Creator Studio by AIMD helps people keep showing up.

01Campaign foundation

Make the message consistent across every format.

  • Confirm the campaign title, core caption, CTA, and launch dates.
  • Choose 8–12 voices across artists, creators, athletes, performers, writers, and enthusiasts.
  • Create a shared folder for raw video, selects, quote cards, releases, and final exports.
  • Assign an interviewer, camera/editor support, approver, and posting owner.

02Creator interviews

Capture short, honest answers that can become a series.

  • Record a clean 15-second answer: “Why does creating a habit matter to you?”
  • Ask: What was your first creative habit? What changed when you stayed with it?
  • Capture 30–60 second interviews plus vertical and horizontal versions.
  • Pull 1–3 strong quotes, creator name, medium, handle, and permission for use.
  • Capture quiet B-roll: hands, tools, movement, workspace, process, and finished work.

03App proof + previews

Show the real product in use; do not rely only on promotional graphics.

  • Record onboarding, dashboard, habit creation, studio log, profile, and community screens.
  • Show a creator viewing a featured challenge and earning 20 points for an original response.
  • Capture workshop browsing, instructor pages, enrollment, and event details.
  • Film real testers using the app with permission; include reactions and quick quotes.
  • Export short Reels, Stories, stills, and one clean product walkthrough.

04Workshop content

Give every workshop enough material to explain its value.

  • Record each instructor’s 15–30 second welcome and workshop promise.
  • Capture the skill, outcome, who it is for, and what participants will make or practice.
  • Film one short drill or teaching moment from each workshop.
  • Collect instructor bio, headshot, workshop image, date, location, and signup CTA.
  • Prepare one announcement, one reminder, and one last-call post per workshop.

05Anticipation build-up

Build recognition before asking people to attend or download.

  • Save-the-date post and campaign introduction.
  • Three-week, two-week, one-week, and 72-hour countdown beats.
  • Creator quote series, instructor spotlights, app previews, and behind-the-scenes setup.
  • Partner and collaborator repost kit with approved copy, images, and links.
  • Email, AIMD SMS list, website, in-app broadcast, Instagram, and event reminders.

06Event capture plan

Make the launch event a source of future content, not only a single day.

  • Assign a photo lead, video lead, interview lead, and live-posting owner.
  • Capture arrival, setup, welcome, workshops, app use, creator quotes, and community moments.
  • Collect attendee first-habit stories and 15-second “what I made” clips.
  • Capture signage, QR codes, workshop tables, instructor interactions, and final group moments.
  • Plan next-day recap, quote cards, gallery, thank-you message, and follow-up CTA.

07Approval + publishing control

  • Get written permission for every interview, image, voice, and event appearance; confirm name and social handle spelling.
  • Keep a simple content tracker with asset, format, owner, approval status, publish date, channel, and link.
  • Use the same CTA everywhere: Build a creative habit. Join Creator Studio by AIMD.
  • Review comments and questions after publishing; save audience language for future campaign content.

Minimum launch asset set

1 campaign video · 8–12 creator clips · 4 quote cards · 1 product walkthrough · 2–3 workshop promos · event save-the-date · launch recap.

Weekly content check

What was captured? What is approved? What publishes next? What is missing? Who owns the next action?
