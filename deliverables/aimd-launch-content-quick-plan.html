Lead: Anthony First launch content pass

Goal: Show why creative habits matter, then show the range of people and practices Creator Studio by AIMD supports.

Campaign · Why building a creative habit matters

Capture 8–12 short creator clips. Each person shares a quick answer about why practicing, returning, and building a creative habit helps them maintain their creativity.

01Creator clips

These do not need to be full interviews. Keep them natural, direct, and easy to edit.

  • Length: 15–30 seconds each.
  • Prompt: “Why is building a creative habit important to you?”
  • Use spoken answers, written quotes, or quote cards with a photo.
  • Include a mix of artists, creators, athletes, performers, and enthusiasts.
  • Capture the person creating, practicing, training, or working in their medium.
  • Get permission to use their name, image, voice, quote, and social handle.

02Starter modules

Build four to six short lessons. Start with these five people and add one more if needed.

Anthony · Tattoo preparation Set up and prep the tattoo station; include a brief version of his journey and approach.
David Singleton · Basketball A practical basketball lesson built around practice, repetition, and discipline.
Saeed · Vocal lessons A simple vocal exercise or lesson creators can practice.
Ali · Workout A focused workout lesson connected to consistency and building a routine.
Ralph · Drumming An accessible drumming lesson or rhythm practice.

Next actions

Confirm the five module participants · choose the additional clip participants · schedule simple recording sessions · capture and edit the 8–12 habit clips · record the five starter lessons · add titles, thumbnails, captions, and short descriptions · upload and review everything before launch.
