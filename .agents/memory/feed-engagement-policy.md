---
name: Feed engagement policy
description: Owner decision to use private messages instead of public likes and comments on feed posts.
---

Feed posts should not have public likes, reaction counts, or comments. Creator-to-creator feedback should happen through direct messages.

**Why:** On October 12, 2026, the owner asked to remove likes and comments from the feed because “we want people to only be able to direct messeage each other.”

**How to apply:** Keep mobile and web feed behavior consistent. Do not reintroduce public engagement controls as a growth feature without the owner's approval. Preserve reporting/blocking and existing records rather than treating removal of a feature as permission to delete historical data.
