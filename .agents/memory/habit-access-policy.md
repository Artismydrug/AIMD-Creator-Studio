---
name: Habit access policy
description: Product decision for habit creation and tracking access across membership tiers
---

Habit creation and tracking are unlimited for every membership tier, including the free plan. Habit limits should not be used as a membership conversion gate.

**Why:** The creator practice system is intended to support artists at every stage, and the product owner explicitly decided that habits do not need a free-plan limit.

**How to apply:** Keep habit CRUD and session logging available to free creators. Paid tiers may differentiate on other benefits, but not on the number of habits.
