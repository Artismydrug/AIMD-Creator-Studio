---
name: Habit access policy
description: Product decision for habit creation and tracking access across membership tiers
---

Habit creation and tracking are unlimited for every membership tier, including the free plan. Habit limits should not be used as a membership conversion gate.

**Why:** The creator practice system is intended to support artists at every stage, and the product owner explicitly decided that habits do not need a free-plan limit.

**How to apply:** Keep habit CRUD and session logging available to free creators. Paid tiers may differentiate on other benefits, but not on the number of habits.

Habits should be the first/default section, with Challenges second, and daily habit practice should take priority over featured challenges on the Dashboard.

**Why:** The product owner wants daily habits to be the primary focus, rather than challenges, and explicitly chose habit practice history instead of the Dashboard's featured challenge.

**How to apply:** Preserve habits-first ordering and default selection when changing the shared mobile practice screen or its navigation labels.

The owner accepted the rolling-week interpretation of the practice graph and confirmed that the baseline-to-progress fire marker matches the intended experience.

**Why:** On October 10, 2026, the owner said “that looks perfect” after the weekly interpretation was clarified and the marker presentation was updated.

**How to apply:** Treat the weekly history view as an approved product choice, not an accidental implementation detail. Do not silently replace it with a daily-status chart during future visual refinements.
