---
name: Launch content policy
description: User-approved rules for keeping the public AIMD launch experience credible.
---

Do not show fake content, demo-only interfaces, unavailable checkout flows, or "coming soon" messaging on public launch surfaces, except for the workshop preview exception below. Keep form placeholders that help users enter real information and empty states that truthfully explain the absence of user-created content.

**Why:** The creator wants AIMD to feel launch-ready, not like a work in progress, while preserving clear guidance in real flows.

**How to apply:** Hide an unavailable feature entirely rather than presenting a disabled purchase, cart, or teaser. Write empty states as factual current conditions and leave input guidance in place when it improves completion.

Workshop preview exception: the four mobile workshop examples may use still artwork labeled “Preview coming soon” until their real preview videos are uploaded.

**Why:** On October 10, 2026, the owner explicitly requested preview placeholders or images while preparing to film the workshops.

**How to apply:** Keep the placeholder non-interactive and clearly labeled; never imply that sample artwork is actual footage. Uploaded videos replace the placeholder, and workshop descriptions remain underneath. This exception does not authorize other unavailable launch features or change full-lesson access.

Remove known legacy sample members from the public community while preserving real members and their work. Do not assume that automatically generated handles or incomplete profiles are placeholders.

**Why:** On October 12, 2026, the owner requested removal of placeholder users while retaining real users. Some genuine members have generated handles.

**How to apply:** Verify seed provenance and identity linkage before excluding a profile. Prefer reversible visibility filtering over account deletion unless permanent deletion is explicitly requested.

Members directory exception: owner-designated automated-test namespaces should be hidden from the public Members list even when their test accounts have linked authentication identities.

**Why:** On October 13, 2026, the owner explicitly requested exclusion of automated test/mock handles while preserving genuine sign-ups. An authentication identity alone does not make a known automation fixture a real community member.

**How to apply:** Keep these exclusions directory-only and reversible. Do not delete accounts or change their post/profile visibility as part of a directory cleanup. Preserve ordinary generated handles, incomplete profiles, and names that merely contain “test.”
