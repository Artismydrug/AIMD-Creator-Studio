---
name: Launch content policy
description: User-approved rules for keeping the public AIMD launch experience credible.
---

Do not show fake content, demo-only interfaces, unavailable checkout flows, or "coming soon" messaging on public launch surfaces. Keep form placeholders that help users enter real information and empty states that truthfully explain the absence of user-created content.

**Why:** The creator wants AIMD to feel launch-ready, not like a work in progress, while preserving clear guidance in real flows.

**How to apply:** Hide an unavailable feature entirely rather than presenting a disabled purchase, cart, or teaser. Write empty states as factual current conditions and leave input guidance in place when it improves completion.
