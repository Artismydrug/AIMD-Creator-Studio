---
name: Community publication policy
description: Owner decision to keep community posting immediate for the soft launch.
---

For the soft launch, let users publish community posts immediately. Do not introduce a staff-approval queue or automated screening unless the owner changes this decision.

**Why:** On October 10, 2026, the owner said, “Lets let user post freely for now, if we need to change anything we can do that later.”

**How to apply:** Preserve reporting, blocking, and staff review/removal controls. Treat pre-publication screening as deferred, not implemented or approved by Apple. Revisit only when requested or when a concrete safety/review issue warrants discussing the tradeoff with the owner.
