---
name: Overview pitch positioning
description: Audience and numerical-claim boundaries for the general Creator Studio pitch.
---

The general Creator Studio pitch should describe the app and AIMD mission for schools, creators, artists, and creative partners, not function as an investor financial deck.

**Why:** The user requested a strong, broadly usable product pitch and asked to save numbers that are not yet credible for the business plan.

**How to apply:** Emphasize verified product capabilities and concrete partnership possibilities. Do not invent financial projections, subscriber targets, fundraising amounts, or rollout dates. Keep financial planning separate unless the user explicitly requests it.
