name: Overview pitch positioning
description: Audience and numerical-claim boundaries for the general Creator Studio pitch.
---

The general Creator Studio pitch should describe the app and AIMD mission for schools, creators, artists, and creative partners, not function as an investor financial deck. School/community messaging should include nonprofit youth workshops, mentorship, and creative-grant support as AIMD's community-impact goals; treat availability as program- and partner-dependent rather than guaranteed.

**Why:** The user requested a strong, broadly usable product pitch and asked to save numbers that are not yet credible for the business plan. They also explicitly prioritized these community programs for the schools audience.

**How to apply:** Emphasize verified product capabilities and concrete partnership possibilities. Do not invent financial projections, subscriber targets, fundraising amounts, or rollout dates. Describe workshops, mentorship, and grants without promising universal availability or outcomes; state that offerings depend on each program and partner. Keep financial planning separate unless the user explicitly requests it.

## Presentation format

Creator Studio's investor and schools/community pitches must be separate editable slide presentations, not standalone website projects or domain deliverables.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected both recent web-based one-sheets and said they need two presentations they can send to investors and to schools/general audiences.

**How to apply:** Use slides artifacts for these pitch requests. Keep investor material separate from the approachable school/community message, and provide shareable presentation exports when requested. Do not turn presentation requests into websites or suggest publishing domains.

## Nonprofit reinvestment intent

Creator Studio plans to dedicate a percentage to nonprofit support for community workshops, retreats, and artist sponsorships. The percentage and whether it is based on revenue, profit, or other proceeds have not been specified.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this future giving model in the investor pitch as part of AIMD's community purpose.

**How to apply:** Present the intent as planned, not an established donation policy or existing donation history. Do not invent a rate, calculation basis, partner agreement, or tax treatment. Financial scenarios should disclose when this allocation has not been modeled.
