name: Lean v1 launch scope
description: Keep the first release focused on habits, learning, community, and creator inquiries.
---

For the lean v1 release, defer the legacy reward/dashboard hub, daily reward wheel, Side Quest, separate challenges, member-drop browsing, and tier-based referral sharing.

**Why:** The owner asked to trim non-essential features for a clean release centered on habit tracking, workshops, community work, and client inquiries. Reversible hiding is preferable to deleting earned progress or member content.

**How to apply:** Keep existing links to deferred features safe by returning to Habits or Studio before mounting feature requests. Keep monthly streak progress and its points logic as core habit functionality. Preserve earned accomplishments in one history view under Edit Profile, not duplicate dashboard previews. Inquiry inbox, messages, account deletion, safety controls, membership access rules, and legal/help screens remain supporting parts of the four-tab flow.
