name: Lifetime points and monthly member drops
description: Points keep accumulating; stock-limited monthly package requests include free and paid accounts equally.
---

Creators keep collecting points without redemption thresholds, spending, or resets. Fixed point-based reward offers are retired. Monthly packages are independent of the point balance.

Every signed-in app account is eligible for monthly drops, including Free, $39 and $99 members; do not restrict them to paid subscribers. Offerings may include a shirt or art print, an event ticket, a workshop, or similar items and will evolve.

**Why:** The owner replaced the three fixed milestone rewards with roughly monthly packages to engage members while keeping staff fulfillment manageable. They explicitly corrected “paid members” to include free accounts.

**How to apply:** Allow one request per account per monthly drop, within staff-defined quantity, followed by manual AIMD review and fulfillment. Access does not guarantee delivery. Do not invent stock, seed public example packages, issue fake vouchers, or automatically grant paid workshop/membership access. Monthly windows follow the Los Angeles calendar.
