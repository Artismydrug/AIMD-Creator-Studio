---
name: School learning evidence
description: Privacy and evidence boundaries for school-focused lesson reflections and portfolios.
---

Lesson reflections belong in a private learning portfolio, not automatically on the creator's public profile or community feed. Authorized workshop instructors and administrators can review them.

**Why:** The first school-focused update is an incremental classroom pilot. Learning evidence can include unfinished work and personal reflections; introducing an educational portfolio must not silently publish that material or imply a new public-sharing permission.

**How to apply:** Keep any future public portfolio publishing explicitly opt-in and separate from reflection submission. Treat reflective text as learning evidence, not a replacement for verified recorded practice-drill proof. Canvas/LMS connections, classroom grade syncing, curriculum alignment, and official credit claims are separate work, not implied by saving a reflection.
