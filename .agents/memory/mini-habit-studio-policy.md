---
name: Side Quest policy
description: Side Quest naming, privacy, and local-only scope for multidisciplinary creative challenges.
---

Use “Side Quest” for the app’s short creative activities. This supersedes both “Mini Habit Studio” and “Creator Arcade” in app-facing UI.

The user chose a Dashboard entry plus a 60-second Flash Sketch to start. Side Quests may rotate across creative mediums, but saved sketches remain device-local and account-isolated. The interface supports text, photo, and short audio prompts; unsaved media is session-only. Activities do not award points, complete habits, or change streaks.

**Why:** The user later renamed Creator Arcade to Side Quest and approved multidisciplinary prompts while keeping the first activity local-only and separate from rewards.

**How to apply:** Keep Side Quests distinct from official habit check-ins and rewards unless the user explicitly changes that policy. Do not imply that session-only photos/audio or device-local sketches are cloud-backed or shared.

Side Quest's purpose is to warm up creative thinking, spark motivation, and reduce the friction of starting, as a bridge toward long-term practice under “Discipline Eats Talent.”

**Why:** The owner supplied this core philosophy for the partner deck and proposed Legend Quizzes about the daily discipline behind excellence across sports and creative practice.

**How to apply:** Describe these as intended benefits, not proven retention or learning outcomes. Treat Legend Quizzes and additional medium-specific activities as planned until implemented. Include music, movement/fitness, fashion, writing, and visual art in the broader creative scope.
