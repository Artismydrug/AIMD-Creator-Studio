---
name: Creator Arcade policy
description: Creator Arcade naming and local-only scope for the first creative minigame.
---

Use “Creator Arcade” for the app’s short creative activities. This supersedes the earlier “Mini Habit Studio” name.

The user chose a Dashboard entry plus the 60-second Flash Sketch as the first release. Saved sketches are device-local and account-isolated. This initial activity does not award bonus points, complete habits, or change streaks.

**Why:** The user explicitly renamed the feature to Creator Arcade after choosing the single-activity starting scope and local sketch saving without bonus points.

**How to apply:** Keep future mini-habit activities distinct from official habit check-ins and rewards unless the user explicitly expands that policy. Do not imply device-local sketches are cloud-backed or shared.

Creator Arcade's purpose is to warm up creative thinking, spark motivation, and reduce the friction of starting, as a bridge toward long-term practice under “Discipline Eats Talent.”

**Why:** The owner supplied this core philosophy for the partner deck and proposed Legend Quizzes about the daily discipline behind excellence across sports and creative practice.

**How to apply:** Describe these as intended benefits, not proven retention or learning outcomes. Treat Legend Quizzes and additional medium-specific activities as planned until implemented. Include music, movement/fitness, fashion, writing, and visual art in the broader creative scope.
