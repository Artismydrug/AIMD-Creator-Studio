---
name: Creator tester feedback
description: Early testers value clear navigation and historical comparisons for habit tracking.
---

Early tester feedback has praised the app's straightforward navigation and asked for an easy way to track habits and compare creative practice over time.

**Why:** Testers said they could find their way around comfortably, while also wanting to see their habit progress across weeks.

**How to apply:** Keep the core navigation short and clear. Make practice history and time-based comparisons easy to find without adding limits to habit creation or tracking.
