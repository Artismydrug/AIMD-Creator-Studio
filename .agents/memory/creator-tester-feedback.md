---
name: Creator tester feedback
description: Early testers value clear navigation and historical comparisons for habit tracking.
---

Early tester feedback has praised the app's straightforward navigation and asked for an easy way to track habits and compare creative practice over time. Another tester had trouble finding how to add habit photos and delete a habit.

**Why:** Compact camera, photo-library, and trash icons are not self-explanatory to every first-time tester, so those actions should be named and their effects made clear.

**How to apply:** Keep navigation short, but use visible Camera, Photos, and Delete labels plus a brief photo-proof/logging instruction. Make practice history and time-based comparisons easy to find without adding limits to habit creation or tracking.
