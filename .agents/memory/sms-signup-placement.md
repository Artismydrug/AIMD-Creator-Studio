---
name: SMS signup placement
description: Product decision for where the public AIMD text-list signup should live.
---

Keep the public AIMD text-list form embedded directly on the homepage. Do not require login and do not present it as a standalone marketing page. Existing or campaign-specific `/text-club` links may remain only as compatibility redirects to the homepage form.

**Why:** The user explicitly prefers a low-friction homepage signup rather than sending visitors to another page or implying that an account is required.

**How to apply:** Point navigation, About, footer, and future QR campaigns to the homepage `#text-club` section while preserving the `source` query parameter.
