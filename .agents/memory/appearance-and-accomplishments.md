---
name: Appearance and accomplishment history
description: Product intent for selectable appearance and access to older earned milestones.
---

Creators must be able to select Light, Dark, or System appearance and revisit their complete earned milestone history, not only a recent preview or current streak.

**Why:** Tester feedback requested dark mode and access to past accomplishments. An expired streak must not make previously earned accomplishments disappear.

**How to apply:** Keep history accessible from the creator’s home/profile and retain earned dates and milestone descriptions. Do not treat a small recent-badges preview as the full history.

Appearance preference is device-local, with System as the initial choice.

**Why:** Different devices can have different appearance needs; this display preference does not require an account or server mutation.

**How to apply:** Preserve an explicit choice across launches and continue following device changes when System is selected.
