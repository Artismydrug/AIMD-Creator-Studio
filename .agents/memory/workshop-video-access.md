---
name: Workshop video access
description: Public lead-magnet teasers, full lesson entitlement, and participant access boundaries.
---

Public visitors can watch uploaded module/workshop preview clips for free. Full lessons require an active $39 Dedicated Artist subscription or enrollment as a workshop participant. Assigned teaching staff and admins can manage their curriculum without buying a subscription.

**Why:** The creator explicitly requested free previews as lead magnets, with full lessons strictly restricted to subscribers and enrolled workshop participants.

**How to apply:** Enforce full lesson access on the server and keep full files private. Locked viewers need a clear membership prompt; membership checkout remains subject to the separate paid-launch policy in `paid-membership-rollout.md`.

Subscription-based self-enrollment is progress tracking, not an independent participant access grant. Workshop participant access must be granted by authorized staff for that particular workshop; completed participants can review lessons, while paused participant access is revoked.

**Why:** Otherwise a subscriber could self-enroll in every workshop, cancel, and retain full access indefinitely. Past workshop attendees should still be able to review the workshop they actually attended.

**How to apply:** Keep subscription enrollments distinct from staff-granted participant enrollments, and never use a stored membership tier alone as proof of an active subscription.
