---
name: Design exploration isolation
description: Protect the current app while comparing preview-only visual alternatives.
---

New visual concepts and design variations must remain in the isolated sandbox and on the shared Canvas board until the user explicitly approves integration. After approval, preserve existing app content and behavior, and apply the selected direction only to the agreed screen scope; Sticker Jam is approved for the mobile dashboard, not as a blanket re-theme of every route.

**Why:** The user wants to protect the established app and its materials while deciding on a visual direction. Approval applies to the selected design, not to changing product content or unrelated screens.

**How to apply:** Keep the existing app and production routes unchanged while exploring. Present independently labeled alternatives together for comparison, then integrate only the approved direction into the agreed screen scope, preserving data, copy, and interactions.
