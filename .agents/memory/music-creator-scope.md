---
name: Music creator scope
description: Music and vocal practice are intended creative disciplines in Creator Studio.
---

Creator Studio is intended to support music artistry and vocal practice alongside visual creative disciplines.

**Why:** The user plans vocal lessons and music-artistry instruction, and explicitly requested that musicians be able to share their tracks as ordinary Studio posts.

**How to apply:** Treat music as shareable creative work in the same feed, profile, and community interaction model as other disciplines. Do not restrict ordinary audio sharing to private instructor feedback or challenge proof.
