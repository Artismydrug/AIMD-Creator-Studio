name: Primary navigation priority
description: Habits-first app hierarchy and the role of Your Studio.
---

The primary journey is daily habit check-in and streaks, then workshops and learning, then community inspiration, then the member's own Studio.

**Why:** The owner explicitly prioritized immediate daily practice over opening on the general dashboard and requested fewer repeated headings and wrappers without losing existing capabilities.

**How to apply:** Keep daily habits as the cold-launch destination. Your Studio is the personal home for portfolio, inquiry inbox, and editing/settings. During the lean v1 release, the legacy dashboard tools are deferred rather than becoming another primary destination; see [launch scope](v1-launch-scope.md). Preserve the community feed's scroll-to-expand behavior.
