---
name: Creative UI copy density
description: Product preference for translating repetitive feature copy into concise, visual cues.
---

Prefer short, action-oriented labels paired with familiar icons, artwork, or visual hierarchy over dense lists of feature descriptions.

**Why:** The creator wants AIMD to feel like a creative studio rather than a word-heavy product brochure, with a premium, distraction-free cream appearance in light mode and important detail retained where users need it.

**How to apply:** On high-traffic mobile and membership surfaces, use a concise description plus a small number of icon-led benefit cues. Prefer calm, themed supporting-card surfaces and reserve strong color for key actions or hero artwork. Preserve the user's appearance choice and dark mode. Keep explanatory copy for consequential decisions, accessibility labels, errors, and legal terms.

Keep primary app headers visually consistent with the Habits & Challenges format: an icon tile, title, and short subtitle. Cleanup should remove redundant UI and copy, not functional features or navigation.

**Why:** The creator requested consistent headers across the app and explicitly chose duplicate/unnecessary UI and copy cleanup rather than broader feature removal.

**How to apply:** Preserve useful account actions, safety controls, access explanations, and context-specific subpage headings. Do not treat every repeated navigation label as redundant.
