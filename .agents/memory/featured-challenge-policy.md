---
name: Featured challenge policy
description: Product rules for challenge cadence, inclusion, points, and sponsor involvement.
---

Use broad, medium-inclusive featured themes rather than discipline-specific weekly assignments. Launch with one challenge every two weeks, while allowing spontaneous challenge drops when there is a strong idea, event, or sponsor.

Every creator earns 20 AIMD points once for submitting an original response. Do not add point bonuses for captions, process stories, feedback, votes, popularity, or winner selection.

**Why:** A fixed weekly schedule creates unnecessary production pressure, and discipline-specific prompts can exclude athletes, performers, writers, musicians, and other creators. Equal submission points keep participation fair across mediums.

**How to apply:** Write prompts around themes and explicitly welcome any creative medium. Sponsors may provide prizes or recognition, but sponsorship must not alter the standard 20-point award or make popularity determine point value.
